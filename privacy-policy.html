Privacy Policy

Gali Cricket Chase 3D (Android package com.galicricket.chase3d)
Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what information the game Bat & Tin: Cricket Golf, formerly Gali Cricket Chase 3D ("the game", "we", "us") uses and why. We built the game so that it needs as little data as possible.

1. Your game on your phone

There is no sign-up and no password. Your progress (holes played, stars, coins, marbles, kids, bats, caps, trails, dances and settings) is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may include this game data in your Google account backup (you can turn that off in your phone's backup settings).

2. Live 1v1 Arena and shared challenges (only if you use them)

Most of the game works offline and sends nothing to our servers. Only if you open the Live 1v1 Arena or a recorded-rival challenge, the game:

  • signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication, which creates a random player ID (not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account);
  • sends that ID and your Arena moves to our game server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. The server keeps: your Arena Coins balance (free play money that cannot be bought, sold or cashed out), the day you last claimed free Arena Coins, your place in the match queue, and each match (both players' random IDs, the course, every shot and its result, the winner and the times);
  • keeps the 30 most recent winning finishes (the winner's random ID, the course, the score and the final shot) so other players can replay them as a "recorded rival" or open them from a shared link. Older ones are deleted automatically.

There are no names, no chat, no voice and no photos in the Arena. Other players see only the shots and the result. Cloudflare and Google process your IP address to deliver the service (see their privacy policies: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy, policies.google.com/privacy).

Older versions: versions before 3.0 had Online Duel, Live 1v1 and leaderboards stored in Google Firebase. That data stays until you ask us to delete it.

3. Game analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To see which parts of the game are played, and to find and fix crashes (especially on low-end phones), the Android app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They may collect:

  • app activity: screens and game events such as a hole started or finished (hole, shots, stars), a race or the tutorial finished, an item bought with coins or a purchase completed;
  • an app-instance ID and, where allowed, the Advertising ID;
  • device and app information (model, Android and WebView version, language, app version, approximate country from the IP address);
  • crash logs and error messages from the game, with the graphics setting and the phone's graphics chip name.

This data is processed by Google on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms. Analytics event data is kept for 14 months.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier);
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location (city/country level);
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded ads completed);
  • crash and diagnostic information related to ads.

This data is collected and shared directly with Google under Google's own policies. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, First Pack, coin packs and the Coin Doubler) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether a purchase was successful so it can unlock the item, and the game can restore items that last (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, First Pack, Coin Doubler). Coin packs are used up when bought. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Rating the game

After a good hole the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card (Google Play In-App Review). Google decides whether it appears, and your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

7. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade any personal information. We do not collect contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone, camera or precise location.

8. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet / network state - for the Live 1v1 Arena, ads, purchases, analytics and crash reports. The rest of the game plays offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) - used by AdMob for ads and by Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Billing - for Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Vibrate - for haptic feedback during play.

9. Age

The game is for players aged 13 and over. The first time it opens it asks your age on a neutral screen; the answer is kept only on your phone. If the answer is under 13, the game does not continue. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

For players aged 13 to 15, ads are requested as for a user under the age of consent, so they are not personalised. Google Analytics never collects the Advertising ID, and it stays off until the age has been answered.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your phone: until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Arena data (your random player ID, Arena Coins balance and matches): until you delete it in the game or ask us to (see Delete your data). A finished match is deleted once both players have started another one. Recorded winning finishes: only the 30 most recent are kept.
  • Online data from versions before 3.0: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics event data: 14 months. Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Ads and purchases data held by Google: under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

11. Delete your data

You can have your Gali Cricket Chase 3D data deleted at any time:

  • In the game: Settings > Delete my Arena data. This removes your Arena player (random ID, Arena Coins balance), your recorded winning finishes and your finished matches from our server, and forgets the anonymous ID on your phone. A match in progress counts as a loss.
  • Arena data, and online data from older versions, by email: email galigamesstudio@outlook.com with the subject "Delete my data - Gali Cricket Chase 3D" and, if you can, the date you last played the Arena (or the player name you used in the old Online Duel). We delete the matching online data within 30 days and reply when it is done.
  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game or clear its app data in Android settings.

Analytics and crash data are not linked to your name and are deleted automatically after the periods in section 10. Ads, purchases and Advertising ID data are held by Google; you can manage or delete them in your Google account and Android settings.

12. Security

All data the game sends (Arena, analytics, crash reports, ads and purchases) travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). For online data from older versions, server rules allow each player to change only their own Online Duel data, and only the two players in a live match can read or write its room.

13. Contact

Questions, or a request to delete your data: galigamesstudio@outlook.com

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
